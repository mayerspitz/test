# Play from an MP3 player, phone or any audio device (live input)

Besides the library, YouTube and links, anything that plays audio can be a **source**, and the app sends it to any speaker(s) you choose, with stream-only volume as always.

| How it connects | What you need | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Bluetooth** (MP3 player, phone, tablet, laptop) | **one USB Bluetooth adapter** reserved for players (one more UB500, ≈ $13) | Once: app → Settings → **Connect a Bluetooth music player** → on the player choose **"Home Audio"**. After that, just turn Bluetooth on at the player: it reconnects by itself. |
| **Cable** (any headphone-out / line-out) | a USB line-in adapter, e.g. **Behringer UCA202** (≈ $30), plus a **3.5 mm to 2×RCA cable** (≈ $7) | Plug it in; it appears automatically. |

Then: **Choose music → Live** → tap the player. It plays on that speaker, live. Tap it on another speaker too to hear the same player in two rooms (independent streams, not synced). 🎧 lets you hear it on your phone first.

**Notes**
- Control play/pause/skip **on the player itself**; the app controls which speakers play it and their volume.
- Set the player's own volume to maximum (or near it) for the best sound; use the app's sliders for loudness.
- Only one Bluetooth player streams at a time on the "Home Audio" adapter. Several players can be paired, and the last one that connects plays.
- Live audio has about a 1–2 second delay; that's normal for streaming.
